# Findings Summary

## Executive Summary

Magist demonstrates solid delivery performance (12.5-day average, 93% on-time/early) with improving year-over-year trends. 
**Recommendation: Proceed with 1-year São Paulo pilot** to validate premium product handling before full 3-year commitment.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Delivery Speed

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Average** | 12.5 days | Within Brazilian standard (4-15 days) |
| **Median** | 11 days | Most customers experience faster delivery |
| **90th Percentile** | 23 days | Predictable worst-case |

**Insight:** Competitive delivery times with Brazilian market norms.

---

### 2. Reliability

| Metric | Value | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **On-Time/Early** | 93% | Strong reliability |
| **Delayed** | 6.9% | Better than 8.8% platform average |
| **Avg Early Arrival** | 11.79 days | Under-promises, over-delivers |

**Insight:** Conservative estimation creates positive customer experiences.

---

### 3. Performance Trend

| Period | All Products | Tech Products |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| 2016 | 19.56 days | 16.25 days |
| 2017 | 12.95 days | 12.94 days |
| 2018 | 12.10 days | 12.89 days |
| **Improvement** | **38%** | **21%** |

**Insight:** Delivery improving while order volume grew 355-fold—demonstrates operational scalability.

---

### 4. Geographic Performance

**São Paulo (Target Market):**
- Average delivery: 11.99 days (faster than 12.5 overall)
- Order volume: 5,256 orders (highest in database)
- **Recommendation:** Ideal for pilot launch

---

## Strategic Recommendation

### Proceed with Partnership - Conditional

**Contract Structure:**
- **Duration:** 1 year (pilot), not original 3 years
- **Geography:** São Paulo focus
- **Requirements:** Service-level agreements for premium products (>€400)

### Rationale

**Why Proceed:**
- Delivery performance meets market standards
- 93% reliability supports customer satisfaction
- Improving trend reduces long-term risk

**Why 1-Year Pilot:**
- Limited data on €540 average product performance
- Market entry risk (Brazil is new territory)
- Flexibility to renegotiate or exit

**Why São Paulo:**
- Best performance (12 days vs 12.5 overall)
- Largest market opportunity
- Infrastructure supports tech products

---

## Risk Assessment

**Low Risk:**
- ✅ 93% on-time rate
- ✅ Improving 38% since 2016
- ✅ Tech products perform well

**Moderate Risk:**
- ⚠️ Premium product performance untested
- ⚠️ 10% experience delays >23 days
- ⚠️ Service mix unknown

**Mitigation:**
- 1-year pilot limits exposure
- SLAs provide performance guarantees
- Monthly reviews enable intervention

**Primary (Delivery):**
- Average ≤12 days
- On-time rate ≥90%
- Delay rate ≤7%

**Secondary (Business):**
- Positive month-over-month growth
- Customer satisfaction ≥4.0/5.0
