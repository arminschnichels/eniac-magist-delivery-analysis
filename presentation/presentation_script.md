# Delivery Performance Presentation Script

**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Presenter:** Armin Schnichels

---

## Script

*"I analyzed delivery performance for tech products on Magist."*

*"Three key findings:"*

*"First, average delivery is 12.5 days—competitive with Brazil's 4-to-15 day e-commerce standard(source: techinbrazil.com). The median is even better at 11 days."*

*"Second, reliability is strong. 93% of orders arrive on-time or early, with only 7% delayed. Magist sets conservative delivery estimates and consistently beats them by an average of 12 days."*

*"Third, performance is improving. Delivery times dropped 38% overall and 21% for tech products from 2016 to 2018, even as order volume grew 355-fold."*

*"My recommendation: Proceed with a one-year pilot focused on São Paulo, where delivery averages 12 days with the highest order volume. This validates Magist's performance with our premium products before committing long-term."*

---

## Q&A Preparation

**Q: What about premium products specifically?**

*"We have limited data on products over €400. That's why I recommend service-level agreements for premium products in the contract. The pilot validates performance with Eniac's €540 average."*

---

**Q: Why only São Paulo?**

*"São Paulo shows best performance—12 days average with over 5,000 monthly orders. It's Brazil's largest market with best infrastructure. Focusing here proves the model before expanding."*
